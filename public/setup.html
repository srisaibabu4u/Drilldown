Set up the team database

Creates the tables in your Supabase database and loads the synthetic demo team and help requests. You only need to do this once.

  1. In Netlify, set DATABASE_URL and a SETUP_KEY of your choice (12+ characters), then redeploy.
  2. Enter the same SETUP_KEY here and tap the button.
  3. Afterwards, delete SETUP_KEY in Netlify to switch this page off.
